01 / MODEL

Network reality becomes evidence before it becomes a score.

Advanced Fabric does not score the network directly. Collectors create versioned measurements; those measurements become scoped evidence; only compatible evidence contributes to an assessment.

04 / PRIVACY CONTRACT

Public by derivation, not by redaction.

Published

  • Aggregate resource and state counts
  • Readiness booleans and evidence counts
  • Observation timestamps
  • 12-character SHA-256 fingerprints

Never published

  • Node, peer, namespace, or resource names
  • IP addresses, prefixes, interfaces, or AS paths
  • UIDs, resource versions, labels, or annotations
  • Secrets, credentials, logs, or Kubernetes object bodies

Hash construction: SHA-256("advfab.org/public-v1\0" + identifier)[0:12]. Domain separation prevents these fingerprints from matching hashes made for another publication. Short hashes are pseudonyms, not anonymity; low-entropy identifiers are therefore omitted rather than hashed.
